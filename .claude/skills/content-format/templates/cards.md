:::: {.flashcard #card-blocks-and-mining-901}
Why does changing an old transaction invalidate every later block?

::: back
Its block's hash changes, and each later block commits to the hash of the block before it.
:::
::::

:::: {.flashcard #card-blocks-and-mining-902}
How often does the mining difficulty adjust?

::: back
Every 2016 blocks, which aims at about two weeks.
:::
